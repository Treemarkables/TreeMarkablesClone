Tree pruning questions

When is the best time to prune?

Deadwood can come off year-round. Structural work is planned around species and flowering. We advise on site.

Will pruning hurt the tree?

Correct cuts at the branch collar heal cleanly. Topping or hacking does the damage — we do not do that.

Do you prune near powerlines?

We work around lines with the right gear and will tell you if a lines company needs to be involved.
