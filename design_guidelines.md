# Tree Removal Service Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from professional service websites like landscaping and home services (ServiceTitan, HomeAdvisor style) combined with nature-focused branding. This service-oriented business needs to convey trust, professionalism, and expertise while maintaining an approachable, local business feel.

**Key Design Principles**: 
- Professional credibility with warm approachability
- Clear service differentiation and process transparency
- Strong calls-to-action for quote requests
- Mobile-first responsive design for on-the-go customers

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette
**Primary Colors (Dark Mode)**: 
- Vibrant Orange: 25 85% 55% (primary brand)
- Deep Blue: 215 80% 45% (secondary)
- Charcoal: 210 10% 15% (backgrounds)
- Warm White: 45 15% 95% (text/contrast)

**Primary Colors (Light Mode)**:
- Rich Orange: 20 90% 48% (primary brand)
- Professional Blue: 215 85% 35% (secondary)
- Clean White: 0 0% 98% (backgrounds)
- Rich Charcoal: 210 15% 20% (text)

**Accent Colors**: Warm amber (35 85% 55%) used sparingly for emergency/urgent CTAs only.

### B. Typography
**Font Families**: 
- Primary: Inter (headings and UI elements)
- Secondary: Source Sans Pro (body text and descriptions)
- Accent: Merriweather (testimonials if added)

**Hierarchy**: 
- H1: 3rem/2.5rem (desktop/mobile), bold
- H2: 2.25rem/2rem, semibold  
- H3: 1.5rem/1.25rem, medium
- Body: 1rem, regular
- Small text: 0.875rem

### C. Layout System
**Spacing Units**: Tailwind spacing of 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 for consistent rhythm
- Component padding: p-6 to p-8
- Section margins: my-12 to my-16
- Element spacing: gap-4 to gap-6
- Container max-width: max-w-6xl with mx-auto

### D. Component Library

**Navigation**: Clean header with logo, minimal menu, prominent quote CTA button

**Hero Section**: Large background image with overlay, concise headline about tree removal expertise, subtitle about safety/certification, primary CTA button

**Service Cards**: Clean cards with icons, service names, brief descriptions, arranged in responsive grid

**Process Steps**: Numbered timeline/steps layout with icons and descriptions

**Contact Section**: Multiple contact methods (phone, email, service area) with clear visual hierarchy

**Buttons**: 
- Primary: Solid forest green with white text
- Secondary: Outline style with green border
- CTA: Slightly larger with subtle shadow

### E. Images
**Hero Image**: Large, professional photo of tree removal team in action or well-maintained property (full-width background)

**Service Icons**: Simple, professional icons for expertise, safety, licensing (use Heroicons)

**Process Icons**: Numbered step indicators or assessment/solution icons

**Team/Equipment Photo**: Optional secondary image showing professional equipment or certified team

**Background Treatments**: Subtle texture or pattern in natural tones, potential gradient overlays on hero section (forest green to darker green)

## Content Structure
- **Hero**: Company intro with clear tree removal focus
- **Why Choose Us**: 3-column grid (Expertise, Safety, Licensed/Insured)  
- **Services**: 3 key services (Hazardous, Emergency, Precision removal)
- **Process**: 3-step assessment and solution approach
- **Contact**: Email, phone, service area with map reference
- **Footer**: Basic company info and additional contact details

This design balances professional service credibility with approachable local business warmth, using natural colors and clear information hierarchy to build trust and drive quote requests.
