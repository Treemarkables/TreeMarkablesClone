### Step 1: Setting Up the Landing Page Structure

**1. Headline and Subheadline:**

* **Headline:** Make it clear and exciting. For example: "Kickstart Your Summer: Win Up to \$1000 Back on Your Tree Care!"
* **Subheadline:** Add a line that explains the offer briefly: "Book any tree care service in the next month and stand a 1-in-90 chance to win \$1000 off."

**2. Call to Action (CTA):**

* Place a prominent "Book Now" or "Get Your Quote" button near the top.
* Ensure the CTA is repeated at least twice on the page—once above the fold and once at the bottom.

**3. Offer Details:**

* **Section:** Explain how the offer works. For instance: "Any job booked between \[start date] and \[end date] automatically enters you to win. We’ll draw one lucky winner for a \$1000 rebate."

**4. Benefits and Services:**

* **Highlight Services:** Briefly list the types of services included (tree removal, pruning, stump grinding, etc.).
* **Show Benefits:** Emphasize why customers should choose you: experienced team, local reputation, 50% growth year on year, and so on.

**5. Social Proof:**

* Include a few testimonials from satisfied customers.
* If you have any "Member of the Month" features or similar stories, consider adding one.

**6. FAQ Section:**

* Answer common questions about the offer, the odds of winning, and any terms and conditions.

**7. Final CTA:**

* End with another strong call to action to encourage immediate bookings.
