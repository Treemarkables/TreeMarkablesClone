## 2. Optimized Service Page Structure

For each service page (e.g., tree removal):

* **H1 heading:** Use the primary keyword + location (e.g., “Tree Removal Services in Gisborne”).
* **Introduction:** A 2–3 sentence paragraph summarising the service and service area.
* **Benefits & Expertise (H2):** Explain why clients need the service, including safety, property protection and regulatory compliance.
* **Process & Safety (H2):** Outline your methods, emphasising safety standards, certified arborists and equipment used.
* **Service Areas (H3):** List the suburbs you serve (e.g., Gisborne, Wairoa, East Coast, Awapuni, Makaraka, Wainui).
* **Call‑to‑Action (H2/H3):** Invite users to request a free quote or contact you. Include a prominent contact button linking to your enquiry form or phone number.
* **Internal links:** Link to related services (e.g., from Tree Removal to Stump Grinding), the About/Arborist Services page, and recent blog posts.

Make sure each page uses keyword‑rich headings naturally and includes at least 500–700 words of unique, informative content.

---

## 3. Site Speed & Technical Improvements

* **Image optimisation:** Compress photos using tools such as TinyPNG before uploading. Use modern formats (WebP) where possible and define width/height attributes to prevent layout shifts.
* **Caching & minification:** Enable a caching plugin (e.g., WP Rocket or W3 Total Cache) to minify CSS, JS and HTML, and leverage browser caching.
* **Lazy loading:** Implement lazy loading for images and embedded content so they load only when users scroll to them.
* **HTTPS & security:** Ensure the entire site is served over HTTPS (get a valid SSL certificate if not already). Redirect HTTP traffic to HTTPS.
* **Mobile responsiveness:** Test pages on multiple devices. Use a responsive layout so text and navigation elements display clearly on phones and tablets.
* **Sitemap submission:** Create an XML sitemap and submit it to Google Search Console. Update it automatically when new posts/pages are added.

---

## 4. Internal Linking Strategy

* **Cross‑link service pages:** At the end of each service page, include a “Related Services” section linking to at least two other services.
* **Link to the About/Arborist Services page:** Provide context about qualifications and experience; this helps build trust.
* **Blog post links:** When writing blog articles, link to relevant services and the Contact page to drive enquiries.
* **Use descriptive anchor text:** Instead of “click here,” use phrases like “learn more about our stump grinding in Gisborne.”

---

## 5. Structured Data & Schema Markup

* **Local Business schema:** Add JSON‑LD schema to your site’s `<head>` section including business name, address (service areas can be specified with `areaServed`), phone, opening hours, and URL. This can enhance visibility in search features.
* **Service schema:** For each service page, specify `@type: Service` with `serviceType: Tree Removal`, etc., to help search engines understand the services you offer.

---

## 6. Ongoing Actions

* **Regular audits:** Use tools like Google Search Console and PageSpeed Insights to monitor crawl errors, index coverage, and site speed.
* **Content updates:** Keep service pages up‑to‑date. Add fresh images of recent projects and testimonials from local clients to build credibility.
* **Check meta tags:** After implementing updates, verify that each page’s meta title and description appear correctly in search results.
