Our Approach to Hedge Trimming

Tailored pruning schedule: We assess the species and growth rate of your hedges to determine how often trimming is required. Fast‑growing pittosporums and griselinia may need trimming twice a year, while slower‑growing evergreens need less frequent attention.

Shaping for privacy and wind protection: Coastal properties benefit from dense hedges to buffer strong winds. We trim to maintain thickness and height while ensuring a natural look.

Cleanup and waste removal: Our team leaves your property tidy. We remove all clippings and can mulch them for use in your garden if desired.

When to Trim

Most hedges can be trimmed in late spring and again in early autumn to maintain shape without stressing the plant. Flowering hedges should be trimmed after they bloom to avoid cutting off buds.
