A **truck pre-start checklist** is designed to make sure a vehicle is safe, compliant, and ready for operation before it hits the road or job site. Here are the **most common checks** included on a professional pre-start inspection form:

---

### 🔧 **Exterior Checks**

* **Tyres:** Correct pressure, tread depth, and no visible damage or embedded objects.
* **Wheels & Nuts:** All wheel nuts secure and no visible cracks.
* **Lights:** Headlights, brake lights, indicators, and hazard lights all operational.
* **Mirrors & Windows:** Clean, not cracked, and adjusted correctly.
* **Windscreen Wipers & Washer Fluid:** Functioning properly, fluid topped up.
* **Body & Panels:** No major damage or sharp edges.
* **Number Plates:** Clean and visible.

---

### 🛢️ **Fluid Levels**

* **Engine oil**
* **Coolant**
* **Brake fluid**
* **Transmission fluid**
* **Power steering fluid**
* **Fuel level**
* **AdBlue (if applicable)**

*(Check for any leaks under the vehicle too.)*

---

### ⚙️ **Engine Bay & Underbody**

* Belts and hoses in good condition (no fraying or cracks).
* Battery secure and terminals clean.
* No fluid leaks or unusual smells.

---

### 🚚 **Cabin Checks**

* **Seatbelts:** Working and not frayed.
* **Horn:** Functional.
* **Dashboard Warning Lights:** None illuminated incorrectly.
* **Steering:** Smooth, no excessive play.
* **Brakes:** Pedal feels firm, parking brake holds.
* **Clutch:** Smooth engagement (for manual trucks).
* **Gauges:** Speedometer, fuel, and temperature gauges operational.
* **Fire Extinguisher & First Aid Kit:** Present and within expiry.
* **Cab Cleanliness:** No loose items that could obstruct controls.

---

### 🪜 **Load Area & Equipment**

* **Load Securement:** Chains, straps, or restraints in good condition.
* **Toolboxes & Equipment:** Secured and closed.
* **Hydraulics (if applicable):** No leaks, smooth operation.
* **Tail Lift / Tipper / Crane:** Operational and safety locks engaged.

---

### ⚠️ **Final Safety Checks**

* **Reversing alarm:** Working.
* **Reflectors & Safety markings:** Visible and clean.
* **Emergency stop (if fitted):** Working.
* **Documentation:** Registration, insurance, and inspection stickers up to date.
